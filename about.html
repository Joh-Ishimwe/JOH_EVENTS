Meet JOH

The planner behind the celebrations.

Celebrations should feel special, not stressful.

JOH EVENTS was created with a simple idea: the people at the centre of a celebration should get to enjoy it, not spend the day managing it.

JOH EVENTS started with a simple observation: the couples and families at the centre of a celebration were often the most stressed people in the room. I wanted to change that — to be the calm, organised presence behind the scenes so the people I work with can actually be present in their own moments.

What I love most is the planning itself: turning a vision into a real timeline, a real budget and a real day that runs smoothly, and watching it all come together exactly as imagined. I work with couples and clients who want their celebration to feel personal and considered, not templated — whether that's an intimate dinner for twenty or a full wedding for two hundred.

Based in Kigali, we work closely with trusted local vendors to bring each celebration together with structure and care.
